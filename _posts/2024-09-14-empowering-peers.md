Today, I had the opportunity to help a friend from academia by going over the DevOps fundamentals—what it is and the tools that enable modern DevOps practices. 🚀 It was a rewarding experience to share insights from the industry and help him prepare course materials for his students.

What I love most about these peer collaborations is the two-way learning. While I help him stay updated on industry practices, he helps me dive deeper into advanced academic concepts. For example, I may request him to go over advanced generative AI concepts if I need to use or implement any such topics in my upcoming projects.

Whether you’re in academia or industry, there’s always something to gain from sharing perspectives and expertise. I’m grateful for these opportunities to learn and grow together!
