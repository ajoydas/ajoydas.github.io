Had a great time at the **Google Cloud Labs** event in Calgary: **"Build Your Own AI Agent with ADK"**!

Big thanks to the Google Cloud Calgary team for organizing this hands-on developer workshop. Special shoutout to Jeff for an excellent workshop — walking us through two hands-on labs — and for patiently answering all my questions. And thanks to Manoj for the recommendation to join!

#### Key Takeaways

As someone currently working on designing and developing **multi-agent systems** using the **LangGraph/LangChain ecosystem**, this session helped me evaluate whether Google's **Agent Development Kit (ADK)** could be a strong replacement (or complement) and how it might improve orchestration, state management, and overall system reliability.

Also enjoyed the presentation of the **Live Agent Build Challenge**, and it was a great opportunity for networking with fellow developers and cloud practitioners in the Calgary tech community.

Looking forward to exploring ADK further and seeing how these **agent orchestration patterns** can be applied to solve complex real-world problems. 💡
