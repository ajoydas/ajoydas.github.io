🎉 Happy to announce that I have successfully passed the Okta Developer – Customer Identity Cloud Certification Exam!

🚀 The preparation journey was both challenging and insightful. This certification dives deep into Auth0 by Okta and how it empowers secure identity implementation across both B2B and B2C applications.

Through this experience, I’ve significantly strengthened my understanding of:
- 🔐 Authentication and Authorization using OAuth2, OIDC, SAML, JWT, Role-based and Fine-grained authorization
- 🏢 Multi-tenant customer identity management using Auth0 Organizations
- 🔄 User migrations and Machine-to-Machine communication
- 🧩 Auth flow extensibility through Auth0 Actions, Forms, and Third-Party integrations
- 🚫 Advanced security practices like MFA, Attack Protection, Backchannel Logout, etc.
- 👤 Topics like- Progressive Profiling, Account Linking, Secure Session Management, etc.

The hands-on portion of the exam really helped reinforce practical application—from configuring login flows and role-based access control, to implementing custom actions and secure API access.

Thanks again to Farmers Edge for supporting my continuous growth journey 🙏!

I’m excited to leverage these capabilities in enhancing our managed services offerings and delivering secure identity solutions to our clients.
