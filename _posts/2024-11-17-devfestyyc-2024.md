Had an amazing time at **DevFestYYC 2024** organized by **GDGYYC** \| Calgary's Google Cloud Developer Group! 🎉

This year’s spotlight was on AI, specifically on Generative AI (like Gemini multimodal models), but there were also fantastic sessions on AR, mobile development, responsible AI, DevSecOps, cloud migration, career growth and certifications, etc.

Huge thanks to **Saqib Ameen, Etienne Caron, Susan Fung, Ameen Y., Norbert Kremer, Richard McCaughey, Kevin Edey, Nav Singh - ਨਵਜੋਤ ਸਿੰਘ ☬, Shubhra Sarker, Ron Koster**, and many others for sharing your knowledge and providing such valuable insights during the sessions.

It was also great meeting **Jason Fallow** at the event! A special shoutout to **Manoj** and **Farmers Edge** for sponsoring my event ticket.

It was great to connect, learn, and get inspired by the vibrant tech community here!

Looking forward to attending more such events. 😊
