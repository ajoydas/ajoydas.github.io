🎉 Happy to announce that I have successfully passed the Google Cloud Professional Data Engineer Certification Exam! 

🚀 The journey to this certification was both intense and enlightening. While the exam felt a bit tough, it was incredibly rewarding as it introduced me to new concepts and technologies in data engineering that I hadn’t explored deeply before.

This certification has significantly enhanced my understanding of:
- ⚙️ Building and Managing End-to-End Data Pipelines
- ☁️ Cloud Data Storage and Processing Solutions
- 🔍 Data Processing and Transformation Techniques
- 🔒 Security, Privacy, and Governance in Data Systems
- 📈 Data Analytics and Visualization Systems

Thanks Manoj and Farmers Edge for sponsoring this certification 🙏 

The hands-on learning experience has been transformative, helping me grow both personally and professionally. I’m excited to apply these enhanced skills to design scalable, reliable, and efficient data solutions in future projects. And looking forward to contributing further to impactful data-driven initiatives!
